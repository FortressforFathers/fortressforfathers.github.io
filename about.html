WHO WE ARE

Born from a simple conviction: fathers and children deserve somewhere to turn.

Fortress for Fathers is a Tennessee nonprofit corporation and an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) organization.

The idea for Fortress for Fathers began in 2021, grounded in a faith-inspired conviction that fathers and their children needed protection, advocacy, and refuge. During 2024, a detailed set of bylaws, governance policies, and program plans took shape with future national expansion in mind. The organization was formally incorporated in Tennessee in 2025 and later recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3).

Our approach is intended to unite early intervention, protection, recovery, principled advocacy, and long-term stability under one mission.

The organizational framework is developed. The next challenge is securing funding, qualified people and partnerships to expand and implement services. Some initiatives are underway while others remain in planning or development.
