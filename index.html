WHY FORTRESS FOR FATHERS EXISTS

Fathers should not have to lose everything before someone steps in.

When fathers experience domestic abuse, a toxic wife, family separation, homelessness, trauma, addiction, or other life-altering circumstances, the resources they need are difficult to find.

Fortress for Fathers was established to help change that reality. We are building an organization dedicated to protecting, empowering, and restoring the life of fathers and their children through advocacy, healing, mentorship, and practical support.

We believe support should begin early enough to help men recognize danger, protect their children, and preserve or reestablish stability for them.

OUR MISSION

Stand beside fathers.
Strengthen their families.

Fortress for Fathers exists to protect, preserve, and restore stability, dignity, and opportunity for fathers and their children experiencing or approaching crisis.

Our vision includes temporary safe housing, advocacy, legal aid, counseling, mentorship, recovery support, and practical resources. Some initiatives are already in development; others remain planned, and not all are currently operating.

SPEAKING PLAINLY

Abuse can be physical, verbal, emotional, sexual, spiritual, financial, or psychological.

Some women engage in physical abuse, including striking a father or child. Other harm can include threats, intimidation, repeated verbal attacks, coercive control, sexual exploitation, spiritual manipulation, or using money and material needs to control another person. Abuse does not always leave a visible injury. Fortress for Fathers recognizes the serious consequences these experiences can have for fathers and children.

Fathers and their children deserve to be heard, taken seriously, and supported in seeking safety and stability.
