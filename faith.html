FAITH & PRINCIPLES

Conviction with responsibility

Our understanding of fatherhood is informed by Biblical principles of leadership, accountability, protection, and care.

OUR FOUNDATION

Faith informs how a man leads and how he treats those entrusted to his care.

We affirm a Biblical understanding of fatherhood and male headship, with leadership tied to responsibility before God.

At Fortress for Fathers, we believe that a father's calling includes protecting, guiding, and caring for his children. We understand leadership as requiring integrity, sacrifice, discipline, and accountability. It is not an excuse for abuse, domination, cruelty, or neglect.

We affirm that a father and his children are a family, even when no mother is present in the household.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Principles we intend to live by.

01

Truth and integrity

Our beliefs call for honesty, integrity, and the courage to recognize harm and seek truth.

02

Duty toward children

A father and his children deserve safety, stability, guidance, and well-being. Fathers should be supported in building and preserving these conditions with consistency and love.

03

Accountability with compassion

We will recognize harms done to men and fathers while also expecting them to examine their own choices, accountability, pursue healing, and act responsibly.

HOW WE SPEAK

We are willing to confront harmful conduct.

Some individuals engage in abusive, manipulative, controlling, or otherwise destructive behavior that harms fathers and children. Fortress for Fathers will speak plainly about such conduct, its consequences, and the individuals responsible.

We believe individual conduct should be evaluated on the facts. We reject the idea that a father's experience of harm must be dismissed because he is a man.

We will pursue protection and restoration for fathers and children while respecting the dignity and safety of them.

THE MISSION IS ESTABLISHED. THE WORK IS AHEAD.
